Why 5 minutes matters

  • MIT / InsideSales.com Lead Response Management Study (Dr. James Oldroyd, 2007; 15,000+ leads, 100,000+ call attempts, 6 companies): “The odds of contacting a lead if called in 5 minutes versus 30 minutes drop 100 times. The odds of qualifying a lead if called in 5 minutes versus 30 minutes drop 21 times.” Going from 5 to 10 minutes alone cuts the odds of contact 5x and the odds of qualifying 4x. Contact odds fall more than 10x in the first hour. Study report (PDF) · archived study page
  • Harvard Business Review, “The Short Life of Online Sales Leads” (Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, March 2011; 1.25 million leads, 42 US companies): firms that tried to reach a lead within an hour were “nearly seven times as likely to qualify the lead” as firms that waited even an hour longer, and “more than 60 times as likely” as firms that waited 24 hours or more. HBR article
  • DBSync’s typical first call came about 9 hours after opt-in, and 46 of 63 leads were never called.

What the contract says, word for word

Source: AGF Pilot Program Agreement between Aletto LLC (“Growth Team”) and Rajeev Gupta of MYDBSYNC.COM (“Growth Partner”). Text is from the Jun 22, 2026 legal-review copy we hold. The signed copy in GHL Documents was not pulled for this page. Clause numbers follow the agreement’s own cross-references (“Section 2” Performance Guarantee, “Section 4” duties).

Section 2, Performance Guarantee: “Within the 16-week term, Growth Team will achieve either 20 additional qualified meetings, or An overall greater ROI from the ad spend Growth Partner is already spending on social media advertising in the trailing 6 months of performance. If not achieved and Growth Partner did not commit a material breach Section 4 duties and was confirmed by objective CRM/tracking data and verified by Growth Team, Growth Team refund your entire investment outlined in section 1 […] Any disputes regarding performance or compliance shall be subject to a 5-business-day cure period after written notice.”
Definitions: “Qualified Meeting: Booked meeting with an ICP-fit prospect who attends. This includes Budget, Authority or direct access to decision maker, Need and Timing.” The guarantee is 20 qualified meetings, not 20 MQLs.
Section 1, payment terms: “Price: $35,000 […] Option 1: $12,500 now, $12,500 1 month later (save $10k) · Option 2: $20,000 (save $15k).” “All Pilot payments credit toward a subsequent Aletto program.” Refund Policy: “Full refund within 5 days of initial payment, less incurred costs.” Late payment: “If a payment is more than 5 days late, services may be suspended until paid.”
Section 12a, ROI Satisfaction Clause: the guarantee is also “deemed fully satisfied” if the client books closed or in-contract revenue of 2x the fees from Aletto-generated leads tracked in the CRM. DBSync reported no opportunities or revenue, so this clause doesn’t come into play either way.

  • Every conversation thread for all 63 leads was pulled (1,076 events). Only 1 lead received a manual outbound message, and it was an appointment confirmation SMS sent 15 h 57 m after the booking (Matthew D., Sep 23).

  • Against the 10-minute rule, the measured result is: no lead has a logged human contact inside 10 minutes, or inside 2 hours. (The “fastest was 2 hours” figure couldn't be reproduced from the CRM; it may come from DBSync's own tools.)
  • Rajeev's Oct 23 email says “our team is chasing each and every lead.” If true, that activity was not recorded in the Aletto CRM as Section 4 requires.
